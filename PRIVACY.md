# OPHunter - Privacy Policy

OPHunter is a personal tool written and used by one person (K Mohith Kannan). It is not offered to the public.

## What it accesses
With the owner's own permission, it reads the owner's Gmail with the read-only scope (`gmail.readonly`): sender, subject and a short snippet of each recent message, including the Spam folder. It never sends, deletes or changes mail.

## What it does with it
- Messages are sorted by importance on the owner's own computer.
- The sender, subject and snippet may be sent to a third-party AI model service to rank them. Message bodies are never read.
- A summary page of the result is published to a private page on the owner's Cloudflare account, at an unguessable address that only the owner knows.
- Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

## Storage
Results and the sign-in token are kept on the owner's computer. Revoking access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions ends all access immediately.
